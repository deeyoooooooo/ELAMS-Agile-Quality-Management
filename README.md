# ELAMS – Agile Quality Management

## Project Overview

**ELAMS (Employee Leave & Attendance Management System)** was developed as a group project using Agile/Scrum practices. The project was completed by a five-member team and focused on developing a system for managing employee leave and attendance.

This repository contains the project documentation related to **quality management and Agile practices**.

## My Role – Quality Manager

I worked as the **Quality Manager** in the five-member Agile development team.

My role focused on supporting quality planning, establishing quality criteria, contributing to testing activities, participating in sprint reviews, and taking part in retrospective activities to support continuous improvement throughout the project.

## Quality & Agile Practices

The project included the following quality and Agile practices:

- Test planning
- Definition of Done
- Quality criteria
- Sprint reviews
- Retrospective activities
- Continuous improvement

## Documentation

The following documents are included in this repository:

- **ELAMS Test Plan** – Test planning and quality assurance documentation.
- **Quality Criteria** – Defined criteria for evaluating the quality of project deliverables.
- **Definition of Done** – Conditions used to determine when work was considered complete.
- **Sprint Review** – Documentation related to sprint review activities.
- **Retrospective – Glad, Mad, Sad** – Reflection on the team's sprint experience and areas for improvement.
- **Individual Reflective Report** – Reflection on my individual contribution, experience, and learning from the project.

## My Contribution

As Quality Manager, I contributed to the quality management process by supporting test planning, defining quality expectations, participating in Agile review and retrospective activities, and contributing to continuous improvement throughout the project.
